UniDic and lindera — the analyser behind the reading passages
============================================================

The word boundaries and the readings the **Read** screen draws over its passages
are one analyser's answer about three short passages. The passages themselves are
written for this course and are this project's own prose; what comes from the
analyser is the segmentation, and it is stored as token boundaries and readings in
`crates/nihongo-core/data/passages.bin.gz`. That artifact is a derivative of the
analyser and of the dictionary it reads, so both are recorded here.

  * [lindera](https://github.com/lindera/lindera) 6.2 — the analyser, MIT,
    Copyright (c) 2019 by the project authors. It runs at **build time only**
    (`prepare-passages`, behind the `tokenize` feature) and is not compiled into
    the app, not bundled with it, and never run by a learner's machine.
  * `unidic-mecab-2.1.2`, through
    [lindera-unidic](https://crates.io/crates/lindera-unidic) — the dictionary the
    analyser reads, three-clause BSD, Copyright (c) 2011-2017, The UniDic
    Consortium.

**Nothing of UniDic's dictionary is redistributed.** The dictionary is built into
`.lindera/`, which is gitignored and part of no bundle; it is fetched once by
`scripts/fetch-unidic.sh` and pinned there by MD5
(`f4502a563e1da44747f61dcd2b269e35  unidic-mecab-2.1.2.tar.gz`), which lindera
verifies before it will build anything. The passages are three sentences written
here, and what their artifact carries is where the analyser found the word
boundaries — not a copy of any dictionary entry.
