UniDic and lindera — the analyser behind the passages, the phrases and the
grammar's word links
========================================================================

Three artifacts carry this analyser's output, and all three are derivatives of it
and of the dictionary it reads:

  * `crates/nihongo-core/data/passages.bin.gz` — the word boundaries and readings
    the **Read** screen draws over its three passages. The passages are written for
    this course and are this project's own prose.
  * `crates/nihongo-core/data/phrases.bin.gz` — the segmentation and readings the
    **Phrases** screen draws over 1,400 sentences imported from Tatoeba (attributed
    in the Tatoeba notice beside this one, which is what the sentences' copyright is
    about; the analyser's part is the same as here).
  * `crates/nihongo-core/data/grammar.bin.gz` — **which of this app's own words each
    grammar example's sentence uses**, so the **Grammar** screen can list a pattern's
    vocabulary and open each word's card. The example sentences are OpenJLPT's,
    attributed in the OpenJLPT notice beside this one; what is UniDic's here is the
    word index, because the corpus's own furigana markup marks readings rather than
    word boundaries.

What the analyser supplies in every case is the segmentation — where the words are —
and it is stored as token boundaries and readings, or as the list of course words a
sentence uses. No dictionary entry is copied.

  * [lindera](https://github.com/lindera/lindera) 6.2 — the analyser, MIT,
    Copyright (c) 2019 by the project authors. It runs at **build time only**
    (`prepare-passages`, `prepare-phrases` and, since N21, `prepare-grammar` — all
    behind the `tokenize` feature) and is not compiled into the app, not bundled
    with it, and never run by a learner's machine.
  * `unidic-mecab-2.1.2`, through
    [lindera-unidic](https://crates.io/crates/lindera-unidic) — the dictionary the
    analyser reads, three-clause BSD, Copyright (c) 2011-2017, The UniDic
    Consortium.

**Nothing of UniDic's dictionary is redistributed.** The dictionary is built into
`.lindera/`, which is gitignored and part of no bundle; it is fetched once by
`scripts/fetch-unidic.sh` and pinned there by MD5
(`f4502a563e1da44747f61dcd2b269e35  unidic-mecab-2.1.2.tar.gz`), which lindera
verifies before it will build anything. What each artifact carries is where the
analyser found the word boundaries — for text written here, text imported from
Tatoeba and text imported from OpenJLPT alike — and never a copy of any dictionary
entry.
