JmdictFurigana
==============

The furigana this app draws over a vocabulary word — which part of a word's
reading belongs over which of its characters — is the alignment published by
JmdictFurigana. It is not derived here: a word whose alignment the project does
not publish keeps its reading and is drawn with no ruby at all, rather than being
filled in by a rule written in this repository.

What this app did to the data
-----------------------------

  * Joined the alignment to this app's vocabulary on `(written form, reading)`,
    which is the pair the project keys on, and kept the alignment only where the
    two agree exactly.
  * Kept the ruby parts verbatim; none is rewritten, merged or re-derived.
  * Re-encoded the result into `crates/nihongo-core/data/words.bin.gz` beside the
    readings and glosses from EDRDG's JMdict.

The release this build was made from is pinned in two places that must agree —
`JMDICT_FURIGANA` in `scripts/fetch-data.sh` and `FURIGANA_RELEASE` in
`crates/nihongo-core/src/bin/prepare_words.rs` — and the release string is recorded
in the artifact's own `furigana_release` field and asserted by
`crates/nihongo-core/tests/words_artifact.rs`.
