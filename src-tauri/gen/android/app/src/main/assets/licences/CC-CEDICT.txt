CC-CEDICT
=========

The word readings and English definitions in this app's dataset come from
CC-CEDICT, a community-maintained free Chinese-English dictionary published by
MDBG. They reach this app through the MIT-licensed "complete-hsk-vocabulary"
compilation, which takes its word pinyin and definitions from CC-CEDICT.

What this app did to the data (the licence requires this to be stated):
  * Selected only the words that appear in the HSK 2.0/3.0 vocabulary lists,
    as compiled by "complete-hsk-vocabulary"; the dictionary's full 125,068
    entries are not included.
  * Stripped the traditional-character headword, keeping the simplified one.
  * Chose one reading per word: the first form listed, unless it is a
    capitalised proper-noun reading and an ordinary reading exists.
  * Left the definition text verbatim; it is displayed as-is and is not
    rewritten, summarised or machine-translated. Supporting text such as
    classifier notation and bracketed glosses is preserved.
  * Re-encoded the entries into a compact binary artifact
    (`crates/hanzi-core/data/hanzi.bin.gz`) alongside the stroke geometry.

Consequence of ShareAlike: the extracted definition text stays under
CC BY-SA 4.0, and so does anything built from it. This does not reach the
program code (AGPL-3.0), the stroke outlines (Arphic Public License) or the
app's own interface text.
