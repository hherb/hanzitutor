Licence and attribution — HSK-graded sentences with audio
=========================================================

Source
------
no7z/hsk-sentences-audio
https://huggingface.co/datasets/no7z/hsk-sentences-audio

What Hanzi Tutor takes from it
------------------------------
The **text** of the graded sentences: the simplified Chinese, its pinyin, its
English translation, and its per-word glosses. Nothing else.

The **audio** in this app is NOT from this dataset. That dataset's MP3s were
synthesised with CosyVoice2-0.5B; Hanzi Tutor generates its own clips at build
time with **CosyVoice 3** (`FunAudioLLM/Fun-CosyVoice3-0.5B-2512`, Apache-2.0).

That choice was made on measurement, not preference. MeloTTS was used first, and
an ASR sweep of 208 HSK-1 phrases found it mispronouncing 57 where this dataset's
own audio failed 15; isolating the recogniser's own errors left **46 phrases only
MeloTTS got wrong** — roughly one in five. CosyVoice 3 says those correctly, and
it can additionally be *told* a pronunciation (see below). The comparison is in
`docs/research/MELOTTS_PRONUNCIATION_ACCURACY.md`.

So the CosyVoice2 line in the upstream attribution below does not apply to what
this app ships — a different CosyVoice version made these clips — but CosyVoice
as a project does, and its licence travels with them.

Changes made by Hanzi Tutor
---------------------------
* Extracted a subset of the sentences, filtered to HSK levels 1 and 2.
* Generated new audio for each sentence with CosyVoice 3, at two speeds. The
  upstream CosyVoice2 clips were not used, modified, or redistributed.
* CosyVoice 3 is Apache-2.0. Its model repository declares the licence in its
  metadata rather than shipping a `LICENSE` file, which is worth stating plainly
  rather than implying a file that does not exist. The generated audio is an
  output of that model, not a copy of the weights, and the weights are not
  redistributed with this app.
* Wrote the sentences into `public/audio/no7z/manifest.json`, which is a
  derived database of the licence text above and remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Because the extracted text is an adaptation, it stays under CC BY-SA 4.0: it may
be reused and redistributed on the same terms, with attribution. The share-alike
condition reaches the text; it does not reach the program code.

**One open question, recorded rather than left silent.** CosyVoice is a
zero-shot model: it clones a voice from a reference clip. The clips currently
bundled were made from the reference recording that ships in the CosyVoice
repository, which was adequate to build and evaluate the pipeline but is **not a
considered decision about whose voice the course should be taught in**, nor a
settled question of what may be redistributed. Until that is decided, this notice
describes the mechanism rather than asserting a right this project has not
confirmed.

A note on the level labels
--------------------------
Upstream grading follows the official 《国际中文教育中文水平等级标准》
(GF0025-2021) and was checked against the community digitisation
`complete-hsk-vocabulary`, which the upstream project itself records as
diverging from the official lists by roughly 1%. Its headline claim that no
sentence contains vocabulary above its own level does not hold: re-grading all
its records against this app's own bundled word list found about 5% carrying a
token above their label. This app therefore does not present the upstream level
as authoritative. See `docs/research/HSK_SENTENCE_SOURCES_LICENSING.md`.
