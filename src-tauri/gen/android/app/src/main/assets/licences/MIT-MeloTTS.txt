MeloTTS — speech synthesis for the bundled pronunciation clips
==============================================================

What this app takes from it
---------------------------
The model weights, run through `sherpa-onnx`'s `OfflineTts` to speak a phrase the
app has no recording for — the **on-device** path, `src-tauri/src/say.rs`.

**It no longer makes the bundled clips.** An earlier revision of this app
synthesised `public/audio/` with MeloTTS; an ASR sweep found it mispronouncing
about one HSK-1 phrase in five (46 of 208 where the reference audio failed 15), so
the bundled audio is now made with CosyVoice 3 at build time. MeloTTS was kept for
on demand speech because it is 53 MB and runs on the `sherpa-onnx` library the app
already links, which CosyVoice — 5 GB and Python-only — cannot do on a phone.
See `docs/research/MELOTTS_PRONUNCIATION_ACCURACY.md`.

The `no7z/hsk-sentences-audio` dataset shipped its own MP3s, synthesised with
CosyVoice2-0.5B under Apache-2.0, and this app does not redistribute those either
— it generates its own with CosyVoice 3. The two-sentence summary is: **CosyVoice 3
makes the clips, MeloTTS speaks live.**

How the model is obtained
-------------------------
It is **not** bundled with the app and is not compiled in. `scripts/fetch-tts.sh`
downloads it to `.melo-tts/` for a build that synthesises clips, checking every
file against a SHA-256 recorded in that script. The app itself never downloads
it: a learner who wants the app to speak an arbitrary phrase uses the operating
system's own synthesiser, which needs nothing.

The clips that *are* bundled are a product of this model, which is why this
notice travels with the app.
